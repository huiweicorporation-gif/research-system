# German dog owners: who they are and what problems they search for help with (2023–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Germany only. Every number carries its data year. Items without a source are in Gaps.

## 1. How many dogs and dog households? Trends, Corona-Hunde, shelters

### Takeaway
Germany had about 10.0 million dogs in about one in five households in 2025, down from 10.5 million in 2023 and 2024 (IVH/ZZF). The pandemic boom was followed by a shelter crisis. In May 2024, 82% of Tierschutzbund shelters reported more animals in their care since 2022, and only 18% still had space. Shelters name owners being overwhelmed, lack of time and bite incidents among the main reasons for giving a dog up.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 (IVH/ZZF, published 27 Apr 2026):** 10 million dogs, at least one dog in "every fifth household" (about 20%). 43% of these dogs are Mischlinge (mixed breeds). In total, 33.4 million dogs, cats, small mammals and birds lived in German homes, and 43% of households had at least one pet. The survey was run by Skopos, household-representative, n = 5,000, by phone and online. — [IVH/ZZF PM "Der Deutsche Heimtiermarkt 2025"](https://www.zzf.de/fileadmin/ZZF/Pressemeldungen/2026/2026_04_27_Marktdaten_der_Heimtierbranche/IVH_ZZF_PM_Der_Deutsche_Heimtiermarkt_2025.pdf)
- **2024 and 2023 (IVH/ZZF):** 10.5 million dogs in 21% of households in both years. All pets: about 34 million in 2024 and 34.3 million in 2023. — [web.de summary of the IVH/ZZF 2024 data](https://web.de/magazine/ratgeber/haustiere/deutsche-milliarden-jahr-haustiere-40885010); [IVH/ZZF PM 2023 (presseportal)](https://www.presseportal.de/download/document/661e3e9f270000fd2cdf018c-zzf-ivh-pm-der-deuts-mtiermarkt-2023.pdf)
- **Dog market 2025 (IVH/ZZF):** Dog food sold in stores came to about €1.8 billion (−0.3%). Dog snacks grew 2.1% to €768 million, wet food fell 1.9% to €582 million and dry food fell 2.7% to €403 million. Dog accessories fell 6.5% to €200 million because owners put off larger purchases. — [IVH/ZZF 2025](https://www.zzf.de/fileadmin/ZZF/Pressemeldungen/2026/2026_04_27_Marktdaten_der_Heimtierbranche/IVH_ZZF_PM_Der_Deutsche_Heimtiermarkt_2025.pdf)
- **Pandemic boom:** According to the IVH, 600,000 dogs moved into German households in the first pandemic year (2020). — [web.de "Bilanz Hunde-Boom", 21 Mar 2023](https://web.de/magazine/ratgeber/haustiere/bilanz-hunde-boom-37938490)
- **Regret (YouGov, spring 2022, pet owners in general):** 25% had problems caring for their pet, 20% regretted getting it, and 8% said they might have to give it up. — [web.de, 2023](https://web.de/magazine/ratgeber/haustiere/bilanz-hunde-boom-37938490)
- **Tierschutzbund on returns:** Since summer 2021 more dogs, especially large ones, have been handed in to shelters, but there are "no concrete numbers". Spokesperson Lea Schmitz called Corona a "Brennglas" (magnifying glass) for problems that already existed. — [web.de, 2023](https://web.de/magazine/ratgeber/haustiere/bilanz-hunde-boom-37938490)
- **Shelter trend survey (Tierschutzbund with Fressnapf, May 2024, n = 218 shelters):**
  - 69% of shelters reported at least very high occupancy, and 49% were full or overfull. Only 18% still had capacity.
  - 82% said the number of animals in their care had risen since 2022, "with the pandemic tailing off and as a result of the pet boom".
  - 74% said more sick animals were arriving. Places also stay occupied for a long time by dogs with problem behaviour, "often through the previous owners' lack of expertise".
  - Main reasons for giving animals up were being overwhelmed, lack of time, bite incidents with dogs and higher vet fees.
  - [Tierschutzbund PM, 28 Aug 2024](https://www.findefix.com/fileadmin/content/Bilder/5_Ueber-uns/Presse/PM_2024/240828_Tierheime_ueberfuellt.pdf)
- **April 2025 (Tierschutzbund):** The same surrender reasons were repeated (overwhelmed, lack of time, bite incidents, vet costs), and still only 18% of shelters had capacity. "Behaviourally conspicuous" animals block places for a long time. The Tierschutzbund demands a mandatory Sachkundenachweis (proof of knowledge before getting a pet), mandatory registration and a ban on online pet sales. — [Tierschutzbund PM, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.findefix.com/fileadmin/content/Bilder/5_Ueber-uns/Presse/PM_2025/250407_Haustierabgaben.pdf)
- **Local example, Dresden 2025:** The city shelter took in 191 dogs. 49 dogs were rehomed, compared with 82 in 2024. Long stays mainly affect dogs. — [Stadt Dresden PM, Mar 2026 (via search result)](https://www.dresden.de/de/rathaus/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/03/pm_019.php)
- **Illegal pet trade:** 515 dogs were registered in illegal-trade cases in 2024, a falling trend since 2022 (Deutsches Tierärzteblatt). For 2025 the Tierschutzbund counted 2,151 animals in total, mostly dogs, and says the trend is rising again. — [DTBl 08/2025](https://bundestieraerztekammer.de/btk/dtbl/archiv/2025/artikel/DTBl_08-2025_Heimtierhandel.pdf); [Tierschutzbund Auswertung 2025](https://www.tierschutzbund.de/fileadmin/Seiten/tierschutzbund.de/Downloads/Berichte/Auswertung_Illegaler_Heimtierhandel_2025.pdf) (both read from search summaries only)
- **"Corona-Hunde" behaviour (shelter reports, 2021):** Shelters in Saarland and Rhineland-Palatinate described surrendered Corona dogs as about one year old and "completely unsocialised". — [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/komplett-unsozialisiert-probleme-mit-corona-hunden-235790)

### Inferences
- Dog numbers peaked during and after the pandemic and are now falling (10.5 million in 2024 to 10.0 million in 2025). This is a mature or shrinking market. The pandemic cohort, born 2020–2022, is now 3–6 years old. That fits the hundeo finding (section 4) that reactivity to other dogs becomes the top problem in 3–5- and 6+-year-old dogs.
- Both shelter press releases name bite incidents and owners being overwhelmed as reasons for giving dogs up. Content like "before you give your dog away" or "Hund abgeben – Alternativen" meets a real need. The autocomplete suggestion "hund kann nicht alleine bleiben abgeben" (section 5) points the same way.

### Gaps
- No official count of Corona dogs handed in. The Tierschutzbund says no figures exist.
- No national count of shelter dogs or surrenders for 2023–2025. The Tierschutzbund annual reports were not readable in this pass.
- The exact IVH household share for 2025 is given only as "every fifth household", with no exact percentage.

## 2. Who are the owners? Age, household type, first-time owners, urban vs rural

### Takeaway
IVH/ZZF publishes demographics only for pet owners in general, not for dog owners. In 2025 about half were aged 30–59 and a quarter were over 60. 26% lived alone, and two-thirds of families with children had a pet. No dog-specific age data, first-time-owner share or urban/rural split was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Pet owners 2025 (all species):**
  - Age: 17% up to 29, 19% aged 30–39, 18% aged 40–49, 21% aged 50–59 and 25% over 60. This was "hardly changed" from previous years.
  - Household size: 26% one-person, 35% two-person and 39% three or more people.
  - 67% of families with children and 28% of singles had a pet.
  - 61% buy pet products both in store and online, and almost one third buy only in store.
  - [IVH/ZZF 2025](https://www.zzf.de/fileadmin/ZZF/Pressemeldungen/2026/2026_04_27_Marktdaten_der_Heimtierbranche/IVH_ZZF_PM_Der_Deutsche_Heimtiermarkt_2025.pdf)
- **Hundeo app, July 2025–June 2026, self-selected:** In a subsample with a plausible birth date (n = 5,099), 34% of dogs were under 1 year (1,733) and 36% were 1–2 years (1,848). This shows that people looking for training help skew towards owners of young dogs. — [hundeo Trainingsreport 2026](https://www.hundeo.com/presse/trainingsreport-2026)

### Inferences
- The two biggest age groups are 50–59 and 60+ (together 46% of pet owners). Many viewers are likely middle-aged or older. Calm, explanatory content probably suits them better than fast-cut youth formats. This is an inference, and the data covers all pet owners, not only dog owners.
- 26% of pet owners live alone. Single and working owners are a natural audience for Alleinbleiben (being left alone) content.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 source found for dog-owner-specific age, gender, first-time-owner share, urban/rural split or income. Statista may hold IVH breakdowns behind a paywall. The full IVH/ZZF study (paid) may also have them.

## 3. Breeds, mixed breeds, Tierschutz- and Auslandshunde

### Takeaway
Mixed breeds dominate: 43% of dogs in 2025 (IVH) and number 1 in the TASSO register for nine years running. The Labrador is the top pure breed. Around 95,000 dogs a year come in from EU countries (TRACES, 2022), and more than 100,000 a year come through welfare organisations. The Tierschutzbund calls "about a quarter" of German dogs coming from abroad a realistic estimate. Eastern-European imports lean towards fear and aggression problems, southern-European ones towards hunting.

### Cited Findings
- **Mixed breeds:** 43% of German dogs were Mischlinge in 2025. — [IVH/ZZF 2025](https://www.zzf.de/fileadmin/ZZF/Pressemeldungen/2026/2026_04_27_Marktdaten_der_Heimtierbranche/IVH_ZZF_PM_Der_Deutsche_Heimtiermarkt_2025.pdf)
- **TASSO register top 10, 2025 (new registrations):**
  - Ranking: 1 Mischling, 2 Labrador Retriever, 3 Golden Retriever, 4 Deutscher Schäferhund, 5 Chihuahua, 6 Französische Bulldogge, 7 Australian Shepherd, 8 Dackel, 9 Jack Russell Terrier, 10 Zwergpudel.
  - Mixed breeds have been number 1 for nine years in a row, and the Labrador number 2 since 2017.
  - Dackel and Zwergpudel are new in the top 10. Yorkshire Terrier and Havaneser dropped out.
  - [t-online, updated 29 Jan 2026](https://www.t-online.de/leben/aktuelles/id_101097240/hunde-ranking-2025-das-sind-die-beliebtesten-rassen-der-deutschen.html)
- **TASSO 2024:** More than 95,000 mixed breeds and more than 15,000 Labradors were newly registered. **TASSO 2023:** about 105,000 mixed breeds, 17,700 Labradors and 9,346 German Shepherds. — [web.de 2024](https://web.de/magazine/wissen/tiere/deutschlands-beliebteste-hunde-2024-mischlinge-labradore-40653818); [leinetal24 2023](https://www.leinetal24.de/leben/tiere/beliebte-hunderassen-deutschland-tasso-mischling-ranking-2023-zehn-zr-92791365.html) (both read from search summaries; the 2024 figure is ambiguously worded)
- **Imports recorded in TRACES (EU countries to Germany):** about 67,700 dogs (2018), about 120,000 (2021) and about 95,000 (2022). — [leinetal24 / KNA, 1 Oct 2025](https://www.leinetal24.de/welt/ist-auslandshunde-warum-hilfe-nicht-immer-gut-gemeint-zr-93962402.html)
- **Imports from Romania, Spain and Hungary alone:** on average "fast 72.000" (almost 72,000) dogs per year in 2019–2022 (TRACES).
  - The article reports that "about a quarter" of Germany's roughly 10.5 million dogs are said to come from abroad. Lea Schmitz (Tierschutzbund) called this "eine realistische Schätzung" (a realistic estimate).
  - Illegally traded dogs may add "possibly several hundred thousand a year".
  - [Nordkurier, 23 Jul 2025](https://www.nordkurier.de/panorama/import-von-hunden-wohnungsknast-statt-strassenglueck-3778876)
- **TRACES coverage is incomplete:** TASSO says there is no clear legal basis that makes TRACES reporting mandatory. — [TASSO Stellungnahme](https://www.tasso.net/getattachment/Tierschutz/Tierschutz-Ausland/Hunde-aus-dem-Ausland/Stellungnahme-Rechtliche-Situation-Hundeverbringung-nach-Deutschland.pdf.aspx?lang=de-DE) (via search summary)
- **Academic study (Graf & Kuhne 2025, *Animal Welfare*):**
  - More than 100,000 dogs a year are brought to Germany by welfare organisations.
  - 764 German organisations were identified. The homepages of 241 were analysed (2021–2023), with on average 77.5 dogs on offer per organisation.
  - Earlier work by the same group found severe behaviour problems "generally uncommon". Eastern-European dogs more often showed anxiety- and aggression-related problems, and southern-European dogs more often showed prey drive and hunting.
  - Only 33.6% of the organisations informed adopters about typical behaviour traits of imported dogs.
  - [PMC12554802](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12554802)
- **Typical problems described by experts:**
  - Fearful dogs that can hardly be touched, snap and pull in panic on the lead. Buyers often misread this as a sign of past abuse.
  - Missing socialisation to humans in the first weeks, which can lead to chronic stress. These problems are often hard to train away. — [Nordkurier 2025](https://www.nordkurier.de/panorama/import-von-hunden-wohnungsknast-statt-strassenglueck-3778876)
  - The Tierschutzbund says imported dogs struggle with city flats, close human contact, unfamiliar noises and traffic. The VDH says they often have "einen Knacks" (a crack, i.e. some damage). — [leinetal24 / KNA 2025](https://www.leinetal24.de/welt/ist-auslandshunde-warum-hilfe-nicht-immer-gut-gemeint-zr-93962402.html)
- **Research in progress:** LMU München is studying how imported dogs develop in their first 6 months in a German home. — [LMU call for participants](https://www.tierhyg.vetmed.uni-muenchen.de/download/aufruf-besitzer-online_10-6-21.pdf)

### Inferences
- About 95,000–120,000 recorded imports a year, plus unrecorded ones, make imported rescues a very large audience segment. Autocomplete confirms their questions: "auslandshund eingewöhnen" (settling in), "stubenrein bekommen" (house training), "probleme", "wieder abgeben" (give back; section 5).
- Content split by region of origin (Romanian street dog = fear of people and noise; Spanish galgo or podenco = hunting drive) has a scientific basis (Graf et al.).
- Breed content with the most reach: mixed breeds, Labrador, Golden, Shepherd, Chihuahua, French Bulldog and Australian Shepherd. The Australian Shepherd, a herding and high-drive dog, and the rising Dackel (hunting dog) map onto the problem topics.

### Gaps
- No VDH puppy statistics for 2024 or 2025 found (Statista holds them behind a paywall). No 2023–2025 TRACES totals.
- "A quarter of all dogs from abroad" is an estimate the Tierschutzbund endorses, not a measured statistic.
- No German share of dogs adopted from shelters (domestic Tierschutz) found.

## 4. Which behaviour problems do owners struggle with most?

### Takeaway
The biggest 2025–2026 dataset (hundeo app, 25,589 German-language dog profiles, self-selected) ranks the problems as follows:
1. Leinenziehen (pulling on the lead), 52.7%
2. "hört nicht" (doesn't listen; the recall proxy), 48%
3. eating things off the ground, 40.6%
4. reacting strongly to other dogs, 33.0%
5. can't stay alone, 32.8%

The top problem shifts with age: puppy problems first, then lead pulling, then reactivity to other dogs. No representative German survey, trainer-association statistic or insurer statistic on behaviour problems was found. Silvester (fireworks) fear is commonly given as affecting "about 50%" of dogs, but no primary German source was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Hundeo Trainingsreport 2026** (profiles created 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Jun 2026, 25,589 German-language dog profiles, 22 categories, multiple answers; self-selected and not representative; the "de" app setting is not a reliable country filter) — [hundeo](https://www.hundeo.com/presse/trainingsreport-2026):
  - Share of profiles per challenge:
    - Pulls on lead 52.7%
    - Doesn't listen 48.0%
    - Eats everything off the ground 40.6%
    - Sit/stay/wait doesn't work 35.2%
    - Doesn't stop at the kerb 34.5%
    - Can't wait calmly 33.7%
    - Reacts strongly to other dogs 33.0%
    - Can't stay alone 32.8%
    - Jumps up at strangers 31.7%
    - Constantly distracted 29.2%
    - Bites or nips (hurts) 28.9%
    - Pees in the flat 27.4%
    - Barks constantly 25.2%
    - Needs more exercise 21.1%
    - Can't relax 20.3%
    - Fearful of people or dogs 16.2%
    - Digs in the garden 9.7%
    - Won't go in a crate 8.3%
    - Rejects the muzzle 3.6%
  - 52.6% of profiles named five or more problems (median 5).
  - Top problem by age:
    - Under 1 year: eats off the ground 53.6%, nips 51.1%, pees indoors 50.9%.
    - 1–2 years: pulls on lead 66.9%.
    - 3–5 years: pulls on lead 54.2%, reacts to dogs 48.4%.
    - 6+ years: reacts to dogs 54.7%.
  - Most common pairs: lead pulling + reactivity to dogs (3–5 years, 29.5%); distractibility + reactivity to dogs (6+ years, 28.3%).
  - The report has no separate categories for recall or hunting drive.
- **Shelter view:** Behaviour problems and bite incidents are among the top reasons for surrender, and dogs with problem behaviour block shelter places for a long time (Tierschutzbund, 2024 and 2025). — [PM 2024](https://www.findefix.com/fileadmin/content/Bilder/5_Ueber-uns/Presse/PM_2024/240828_Tierheime_ueberfuellt.pdf); [PM 2025](https://www.findefix.com/fileadmin/content/Bilder/5_Ueber-uns/Presse/PM_2025/250407_Haustierabgaben.pdf)
- **Silvester (New Year fireworks):** VIER PFOTEN cites a study saying about 52% of dogs show fear reactions to fireworks, but no primary study is named. — [verbandsbuero / VIER PFOTEN](https://www.verbandsbuero.de/silvester-feuerwerk-warum-es-fuer-tiere-zur-hoelle-wird/)
  - Hundeo says "about 50%" without a source. — [hundeo](https://www.hundeo.com/magazin/silvester-mit-hund)
  - In a Statista survey, 41% of Germans call fireworks irresponsible because dogs suffer fear. — [Statista Jahreswechsel 2024/2025](https://de.statista.com/infografik/amp/23700/umfrage-zum-thema-feuerwerk-an-silvester)
  - A Fressnapf survey for 2024/25 found that 48% of pet owners (not only dog owners) say they are well prepared. — [Fressnapf PM](https://presse.fressnapf.de/pressreleases/gut-vorbereitet-ins-jahr-2025-wie-tierhalter-innen-in-deutschland-silvester-meistern-3361408.pdf) (via search summary; the PDF could not be read)
- **Imported dogs:** fear, aggression and hunting depend on the region of origin (Graf & Kuhne 2025). — [PMC12554802](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12554802)

### Inferences
- The "evergreen five" for a German channel: Leinenführigkeit (lead walking), Rückruf (recall; "hört nicht"), Hundebegegnungen and reactivity, Alleinbleiben, and puppy problems (nipping, house training, eating off the ground). All have about 30–53% prevalence among owners who look for training help.
- Eating off the ground (40.6%) and jumping up at people (31.7%) are underrated topics. They rank above several classic topics.
- The shift with age (reactivity rising in older dogs) suggests separate series for "Welpe" (puppy), "Junghund" (adolescent dog) and "erwachsener Hund" (adult dog).

### Gaps
- No German numbers found for Jagdtrieb (hunting drive), Ressourcen verteidigen (resource guarding) or Hund und Kind (dogs and children).
- Nothing found from BHV or IBH, nor problem statistics from insurers (Uelzener, Agila) or TASSO. Agila publishes breed and claims rankings, but no behaviour data was found.
- No primary German study found for the Silvester fear prevalence.
- The hundeo data is self-selected app users and may include Austria and Switzerland.

## 5. Common German search phrases

### Takeaway
Google autocomplete for Germany (fetched 10 Nov 2026) shows that owners mostly phrase problems as "Hund + symptom + was tun" (dog + symptom + what to do) and "warum macht/frisst mein Hund …" (why does my dog do/eat …). Martin Rütter appears as a branded modifier in several suggestions ("rütter"). No keyword volumes or Google Trends curves were obtained.

### Cited Findings
All from Google autocomplete (hl=de, gl=de), fetched 2026-11-10 via [suggestqueries.google.com](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=de&gl=de&q=hund). Suggestions are ranked by popularity but come without volumes.
- **"warum macht mein hund …":** plötzlich in die Wohnung (suddenly in the flat) / in die Wohnung / alles kaputt (destroys everything) / so komische Geräusche (strange noises) / nachts in die Wohnung (in the flat at night) / nicht in den Garten (not in the garden) / jedes Spielzeug kaputt (destroys every toy)
- **"warum frisst mein hund …":** Gras / Kot (faeces) / Erde (soil) / nicht (doesn't eat) / seinen eigenen Kot (his own faeces) / Katzenkot (cat faeces) / Steine (stones) / Holz (wood)
- **"hund zieht an der leine …":** was tun / wenn er andere Hunde sieht (when he sees other dogs) / nichts hilft (nothing helps) / Hilfsmittel (aids) / Übungen (exercises) / und bellt (and barks) / welches Geschirr (which harness) / welches Halsband (which collar) / youtube
- **"hund kann nicht alleine bleiben …":** martin rütter / training / was tun / abgeben (give away) / Medikamente / Beruhigungsmittel (sedatives) / Zweithund (second dog) / Kontrollverlust (loss of control)
- **"hund bellt …":** ständig (constantly) / wenn er alleine ist (when alone) / nachts (at night) / Nachbarn (neighbours) / andere Hunde an was tun (at other dogs, what to do) / Menschen an (at people) / bei jedem Geräusch (at every noise) / Besuch an (at visitors)
- **"hund jagdtrieb …":** abgewöhnen (break the habit) / abtrainieren (train away) / rütter / Halsband / kontrollieren / umlenken (redirect) / Katze / Kastration / Elektrohalsband (e-collar)
- **"hund knurrt …":** mich an (at me) / beim Spielen (when playing) / andere Hunde an / plötzlich Familienmitglied an (suddenly at a family member) / Kind an (at a child) / Besuch an / beim Streicheln (when stroked) / Besucher an rütter
- **"hund silvester …":** böllerfrei (firecracker-free places) / Urlaub / Medikament / Eierlikör (a home remedy) / Gehörschutz (ear protection) / Angst / Hotel / Schallschutz (soundproofing) / beruhigen (calm down)
- **"hund angst vor …":** Gewitter (thunderstorms) / Wind / Fliegen (flies) / Windgeräuschen (wind noise) / Auto fahren (car rides) / Dunkelheit (dark) / Treppe (stairs) / anderen Hunden (other dogs)
- **"auslandshund …":** adoptieren / auf deutschen Pflegestellen (in German foster homes) / wieder abgeben (give back) / eingewöhnen (settling in) / stubenrein bekommen (house training) / Probleme
- **"hund und baby …":** gefährlich (dangerous) / im Bett / zusammenführen (introduce) / aneinander gewöhnen (get used to each other)
- **"mein hund …":** frisst nicht (doesn't eat) / frisst Gras / hat Durchfall (diarrhoea) / Flöhe (fleas) / hat Weintrauben gefressen (ate grapes). Health and emergency queries dominate this broad seed.
- Seeds that returned no suggestions because of an encoding problem with "ü/ö/ß": "hund rückruf", "welpe beißt", "hund pöbelt". These gaps are not evidence of low demand.

### Inferences
- High-intent formats: "[Problem] – was tun?", "Warum macht mein Hund X?", and "nichts hilft" frustration angles (lead pulling where "nothing helps").
- Martin Rütter is the brand to position against or beside: his name appears in the suggestions for being left alone, hunting drive and growling.
- Aversive tools (Elektrohalsband, Medikamente, Beruhigungsmittel) show up in search. That is an opening for evidence-based "why this backfires" content.

### Gaps
- No search volumes (Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Sistrix) and no Google Trends exports were obtained. No German keyword-tool listicle with volumes was found.

## 6. Seasonal peaks

### Takeaway
No Google Trends data was collected in this pass. Seasonality rests on topic logic plus autocomplete evidence for Silvester. The Tierschutzbund names the summer holidays as a trigger for giving dogs up.

### Cited Findings
- The summer holiday can be "the final straw" that leads owners to give their pet up (Tierschutzbund, 28 Aug 2024). — [PM 2024](https://www.findefix.com/fileadmin/content/Bilder/5_Ueber-uns/Presse/PM_2024/240828_Tierheime_ueberfuellt.pdf)
- Silvester-specific searches exist in volume: böllerfrei, Medikament, Gehörschutz, Hotel and Urlaub (autocomplete, 2026-11-10). — [Google autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=de&gl=de&q=hund%20silvester)
- Trainers and media advise starting Silvester training weeks in advance, e.g. "jetzt mit dem Training beginnen" (start training now). — [Utopia](https://utopia.de/ratgeber/silvesterangst-beim-hund-jetzt-mit-dem-training-beginnen_447457/)

### Inferences
- Likely calendar (to be checked with Google Trends DE):
  - Silvester fear: build-up from November, peak 28–31 Dec.
  - Thunderstorm fear: summer ("hund angst vor gewitter" is the top suggestion for "angst vor").
  - Ticks: spring.
  - Heat: June–August.
  - Holidays and Hundepension (dog boarding) or giving dogs up: summer.
  - Puppy questions: likely after Christmas and in spring.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends curves or data on the timing of puppy purchases. Seasonal claims above are unverified hypotheses.
