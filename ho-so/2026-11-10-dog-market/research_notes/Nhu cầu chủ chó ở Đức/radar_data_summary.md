# Radar data, German dog market (own scout run, YouTube Data API, 2026-11-10)

Source: `ho-so/2026-11-10-dog-market/radar/` (niche_dog-psych-de.json, comments_dog-psych-de.json). The raw lines are in
radar_data_raw.txt next to this file. Limits: one day of data, 32 tracked channels, and 908 videos in the 90-day zone
window. This is a snapshot, not a trend.

## Address form: "du" dominates
- The phrase "dein Hund" appears in 138 titles across 31 channels.
- "Ihr Hund" appears in only 18 titles across 4 channels, mostly Gerd Hallmann (AI persona, older-skewing audience).
- "dein / deinen / dir / dich" are all top-10 title words.
- Conclusion: German dog YouTube talks to the viewer with **du**.

## Viewer comments (top comments by likes)
- **Owner responsibility is a strong cultural theme.** On the breed-ban video, the most-liked comments say "Das Problem ist nicht am Ende der Leine sondern am Anfang" and "kein Tier wird böse geboren". Viewers reject blaming breeds and blame owners.
- **AI reception on Gerd Hallmann is mixed, and positive comments get more likes:**
  - "Ein wunderbarer Beitrag – auch wenn ich KI-Videos eigentlich überhaupt nicht mag. Doch hier wird wenigstens was gscheites gezeigt" (31 likes)
  - "Die besten KI Videos, die ich bis jetzt sehen durfte" (17 likes)
  - "Leider nicht echt. Nur k.I." (9 likes)
  - "grottenschlechte KI-Darstellung … unerträgliche KI-Stimme" (4 likes)
  - "Wäre viel glaubwürdiger, wenn da ein Mensch wäre" (3 likes)
  - "Diese Musik ist nicht schön, es stört" (5 likes)

  So useful content is forgiven; a bad voice, bad visuals and bad music are punished.
- **Viewers argue about method.** A reactive-dog Short drew "So ist es nicht ganz richtig!" and "typisch Hundetrainerin – bringt gar nichts". German viewers argue about training methods.
- **Viewers share stories from their own dogs.** This invites a call to comment ("Wie macht dein Hund das?").
- **Some want a printed book.** One comment says they want nothing on the phone or PC, which hints at an older audience.
